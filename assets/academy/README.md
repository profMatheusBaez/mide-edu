# Assets MIDE EDU

Arquivos usados pela interface, armazenados no próprio projeto e disponíveis offline:

- `maker-board.png`: placa com lâmpada e estrelas; ilustração de conclusão.
- `maker-plant.png`: planta sobre livros; decoração da lateral em telas largas.
- `logo.svg`: marca do MIDE EDU (chip com um M e um nó amarelo), desenhada em vetor para a interface.
- `icons.svg`: símbolos em vetor usados na navegação e nos controles.

As duas ilustrações PNG foram geradas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, com transparência RGBA preservada. Os ícones e a marca foram construídos em SVG. As imagens pedagógicas e os diagramas anteriores continuam em uso.

## Prompts usados no ImageGen

### Placa

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative illustration for Arduino Academy educational coding app. Primary request: a charming polished 3D papercraft style teal Arduino Uno microcontroller board tilted slightly in perspective, with a small glowing yellow lightbulb floating beside it and three tiny golden sparkles. Composition: compact horizontal object grouping, isolated, generous transparent padding, no text, no logo letters, no background, genuinely transparent background. Palette: turquoise, deep navy blue, warm yellow, mint. Soft friendly classroom maker aesthetic, clean readable silhouette, suitable on pale sky blue and deep navy UI backgrounds. This is a small supporting decorative asset, not a whole webpage.

### Planta

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative sidebar asset for an Arduino Academy educational coding app. Create one polished charming 3D papercraft illustration of a small lush green leafy plant in a warm golden terracotta pot standing on three stacked books, book spines turquoise, sunshine yellow and orange. No text or lettering. Portrait composition, compact centered full object with transparent padding. Genuinely transparent background. Friendly classroom maker aesthetic, soft lighting, clean silhouette and subtle shadows. Must look good on pale sky blue and deep navy backgrounds. No room, no scenery, no people.
